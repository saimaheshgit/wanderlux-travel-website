Our story

Travel should feel personal.

WanderLux is a travel agency concept built around listening carefully, planning clearly and supporting travellers throughout the decision-making process.

Our mission

Our mission is to make travel planning easier by bringing useful information, practical options and personal support together. Rather than assuming every traveller wants the same experience, we begin with their interests, schedule and budget.

WanderLux values clear communication, responsible recommendations, transparent estimates and respectful customer service.
